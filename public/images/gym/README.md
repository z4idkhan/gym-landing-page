# Gym Images Directory

Place your gym photos here for the Gym Tour page.

## Recommended Images
- exterior.jpg - Gym exterior/building
- reception.jpg - Front desk/reception area
- cardio-zone.jpg - Cardio equipment area
- weights-area.jpg - Weight training section
- group-studio.jpg - Group exercise studio
- functional-training.jpg - Functional training area
- locker-room.jpg - Locker rooms and changing areas
- sauna-steam.jpg - Sauna and steam room
- stretching-area.jpg - Stretching and mobility zone
- cardio-equipment.jpg - Close-up of cardio machines
- strength-equipment.jpg - Close-up of weight machines/equipment
- recovery-area.jpg - Recovery and wellness area

## Tips
- Use high-quality, well-lit photos
- Show off your best equipment and facilities
- Include photos from different angles and times of day
- Consider hiring a professional photographer for best results
- Optimize images for web (compress without losing quality)
- Recommended size: 1200px width minimum for good quality

## Alternative Approach
You can also add images dynamically by modifying the gymImages array in app/gym-tour/page.tsx
to include external URLs if you're hosting images elsewhere.
